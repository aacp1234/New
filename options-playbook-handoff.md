# Handoff for Chief of Staff 10-28: options playbook project

Goal: build with Abe a written, safe options-selling rulebook. Abe is still learning, so it is a living document that gets refined.

## Context
- Abe sells short-dated premium: SPX/QQQ end-of-day credit spreads, 3-day short puts on single names (LRCX, AVGO), and a 30-day wheel / cash-secured puts.
- He asked about a ~99% win rate, running $100k daily, and 25-50%+/yr on a ~10% sleeve.
- Answer given: no guarantees.
  - A $25 credit on $500 of risk breaks even at a 95% win rate; one max-loss day erases about 20 wins.
  - Short-dated options carry high gamma and gap risk.
  - LRCX, AVGO and QQQ are correlated.
  - A short put on a stock is not capped like a spread.
  - Earnings gaps break the model.
  - Planning range for the sleeve: roughly 15-25% in a good year, with 20-40% sleeve drawdowns possible.

## Starting rules (to refine with Abe)
1. Risk 1-2% of the whole portfolio per trade or less; correlated names count as one position.
2. Sell outside the expected move, roughly 10-15 delta.
3. Skip earnings, FOMC, CPI and jobs days unless the trade is for that event.
4. Define the exit before entry (e.g. close at 2x credit or when the short strike is touched); never average down.
5. Take profits at 50-80% of max profit.
6. Log every trade: credit, max loss, result, reason. After ~100 trades, size from the real win rate and average win/loss.

## What Abe wants
1. Develop the strategy document: safe ideas, rules, Allowed / Not allowed lists per strategy (SPX/QQQ 0-1DTE spreads, 3-day single-name puts, 30-day wheel), and per-trade-type instructions (entry, sizing, exit, forbidden). Build on "Cash-Secured Put Rules v7" and the wheel-put-check skill.
2. Review his trades at his daily review, using the Robinhood tracker, the 5 PM email/sheet and the Robinhood connector.
3. For every trade that violates a rule, ask why. Show dollar risk and risk as a percent of portfolio, and say whether it fits the rules.
4. If he gives good reasons repeatedly, discuss softening that rule explicitly and record the change. No silent drift.
5. Track results of rule-following vs rule-breaking trades.

Start by asking Abe which strategies he trades today and what his existing rules are.
